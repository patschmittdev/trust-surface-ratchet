# Results

Cost figures are nominal honest-traffic costs, excluding adversary-generated
traffic; they do not measure total traffic under attack. In an instrumented one-round
N=1000, 40 percent Byzantine, flood-all-shards run, 3,800 deposits and 600 reads
give 448 messages per node, compared with the nominal 320.

> Not regenerated in CI.

Generated by `node simulate --sweep` with the defaults on 2026-09-09, after the
repairs recorded in [`critique.md`](critique.md). The tables are the script's
output, unedited, below the reading guide. A full sweep takes roughly ten
minutes; single runs finish in well under a second.

## How to read the tables

- Two regimes, each with three population sizes. "Memory off" is the regime in
  which honest agents trust the medium; "memory on" is the regime in which an
  honest agent prefers its own observation from the last ten rounds.
- Each cell is the share of honest agents whose exploit choice at the final
  round is the truly best option, averaged over three seeds, under whichever
  of five fixed adversary strategies hurt that cell most. The letter names
  that strategy. Five hand-picked strategies are not the adversary's optimum.
- The reward table under each block is honest reward over the last ten rounds
  as a share of the optimum. It shows wasted effort that the correctness
  figure hides: an agent lured for a round and back the next counts as correct
  and still lost the round.
- The cost column is messages sent plus received per node per round, averaged
  over all nodes. For the sharded medium the average hides that the fixed set
  of replica slots carries a per-slot load that grows with population; see
  the threat model, item 10.
- `broadcast + verify` equals `bound + verify` by construction (one store,
  same random stream); its only distinct number is the cost.
- `attested + verify + global id` is the sharded medium with a cross-shard
  identity accounting switch that the spike does not design. Without that
  switch a Byzantine identity writes once to every shard per round.

## What the tables show

Trusting regime (memory off):

- Anonymous stigmergy, the identity-bound board, and the sharded medium all
  read zero from 5 percent Byzantine at every population size. The adversary
  only has to outnumber the honest explorers on the undiscovered best cue,
  and the honest exploration rate over eight options is about 1 percent of
  the population.
- Conflict-directed verification lifts the identity-bound board and the
  sharded medium with global identity accounting to a minimum of 92 percent through 20
  percent Byzantine (global identity accounting, N=1000, 20 percent), and both read zero at 30. The predicted bound at the
  default verification share is 23 percent; the transition sits a few points
  below it.
- With verification, honest reward is 70 to 92 percent of optimum at 5 through
  20 percent Byzantine across the identity-bound board and global-identity
  sharded medium, against 94 to 95 without an adversary.
- The sharded medium without global identity accounting reads zero from 5
  percent even with verification, under the flood-all-shards strategy.

Verifiable regime (memory on):

- Anonymous stigmergy sits near 50 percent at every share from 10 percent
  up, which is roughly what unaided exploration finds in sixty rounds.
- The identity-bound board with verification holds at 92 to 100 percent
  at 5 through 20 percent Byzantine, 71 to 90 at 30, and falls to 29 to 53 at 40
  to 45 under the spread lure. Reward is 77 to 89 percent of optimum at 5 through 20 percent Byzantine
  (94 to 95 without attackers), and 59 to 61 at 40 to 45.
- The sharded medium with global identity accounting tracks the identity-bound
  board with a maximum correctness gap of 8 percentage points (84 versus 92
  percent at N=60, 20 percent Byzantine), at a nominal honest-traffic per-node cost of 320 that does
  not change between sixty and a thousand agents, against 118, 498 and 1998
  for broadcast.
- The sharded medium without global identity accounting reads 14 to 36
  percent at nearly every cell, with or without verification. It is broken by
  the per-shard identity rule, not by sampling.
